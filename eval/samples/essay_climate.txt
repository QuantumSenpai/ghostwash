The Amazon rainforest stands as a testament to the resilience of nature. Spanning nine countries, it plays a pivotal role in regulating the global climate, supporting biodiversity, and sustaining millions of people.

However, deforestation continues to pose a significant challenge. Between 2019 and 2022, an estimated 11,000 square kilometres were cleared each year, underscoring the urgent need for action. Ultimately, protecting this vital ecosystem is not only an environmental imperative but also a moral one — a responsibility we all share.
