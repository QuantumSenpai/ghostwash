My grandfather kept his tools in an old biscuit tin, sorted by a system nobody else understood. When I was nine he let me hold the small screwdriver while he fixed the radio, and told me not to lose it. I lost it within the hour. He never mentioned it again, which somehow made me feel worse.

Years later I found the screwdriver under the sofa, wrapped in a sock, and I still don't know who put it there.
